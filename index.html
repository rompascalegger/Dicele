HOW TO PLAY

  1. Wheel of Wealth. Tap STOP to stop the wheel. The number is your multiplier. 100 spins again and adds up.
  2. Roll your dice. Tap each die itself to roll it. You start with 5 red dice.
  3. Sixes climb. Every 6 = one floor up and another round. No 6 at all = game over.
  4. YAZZY: most of your dice showing the same number: 5 of 5, 5 of 6 … 5 of 10, then 6 of 11, 7 of 12 and so on. One short? The odd die gets a slow-motion chance (1/6 + wheel luck) to complete it.
  5. Yazzy of 1s unlocks rerolling 1s · 2s the green 1–5 die for 2s · 3s the end-game sacrifice · 4s the d4 shelf: every 4 adds a permanent little d4 that adds chips each round.
  6. Yazzy of 5s = SILVER JACKPOT: your multiplier doubles. Yazzy of 6s = GOLD JACKPOT: a golden d10 joins your table for good and adds its roll to the multiplier every round.
  7. Perfect yazzy (every die the same): one more red die, right away.
  8. Silver 5s: if 2 of 5 dice show a 5 (40%, rounded up), you may SILVER REROLL the whole table. Insta-autoroll takes it whenever there's no 6.
  9. Hand-rollers: roll every die yourself (no insta-autoroll) and the house gives you one second chance per game when you would lose.
  10. Last chance: no 6? The last die is rerolled in slow motion: a fair 1/6 (16.7%) plus your wheel luck of (100 − wheel) ÷ 10 %. Wheel 1 = 26.6%, wheel 99 = 16.8%, wheel 100+ = 16.7%.
  11. Every 5 floors the elevator gives you one more red die.
  12. From 10 dice the Casino Manager puts a bad d20 on the table, one more for every 5 dice. A 1 destroys a red die for good (from floor 25 one more losing face every 18 floors: 1–2, 1–3, ...). A 20 confiscates one of your unlocked bonuses.
  13. 3s, once unlocked, are the last way out: no 6 and the last chance failed? Sacrifice ALL your 3s (destroyed for good) to reroll every other die.
  14. Score = chips × multiplier. At the end you roll a d(total sixes) that adds to the multiplier.
  15. Extras: combos (three of a kind and up, 1–6 straight), random casino events, and falling dice you can tap for bonus chips.
