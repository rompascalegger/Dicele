HOW TO PLAY

  1. Wheel of Wealth. Tap STOP. The number it lands on is your multiplier. A 100 spins again and adds up.
  2. Roll your dice. Tap each die itself to roll it. You start with 5 red dice. Every die is a fair d6: at least a 1 in 6 chance of a 6, however many dice are on the table (bonuses only add to that).
  3. Sixes climb. Every 6 = one floor up and another round. No 6 at all = game over (unless a rescue below saves you).
  4. YAZZY: most of your dice showing the same number: 5 of 5, 5 of 6 … 5 of 10, then 6 of 11, 7 of 12 and so on. One short? The odd die gets a slow-motion chance (1/6 + wheel luck) to complete it. Every yazzy also counts as a 6: one more floor, and you play on.
  5. What a yazzy unlocks: 1s reroll every 1 once · 2s reroll every 2 once with the green die (1, 3, 4, 5, 6: no 2s, and it counts like any other die) · 3s the end-game sacrifice · 4s the d4 shelf: every 4 you roll adds a little d4 for good, and the shelf rolls each round for chips. A yazzy you already unlocked pays 100 × its number in chips.
  6. Yazzy of 5s = SILVER JACKPOT: your multiplier doubles. Yazzy of 6s = GOLD JACKPOT: a golden d10 joins your table for good and adds its roll to the multiplier every round (another one: +10 multiplier).
  7. Perfect yazzy (every die the same): one more red die, rolled right away.
  8. Silver 5s: at least 40% of your dice show a 5 (2 of 5, 3 of 6, 4 of 10)? You get a SILVER REROLL: 2 rerolls this round, so 3 rolls in all to chase a yazzy. Tap the dice you want to keep, then reroll the rest. No need to hold your 6s: a 6 you reroll still counts as a floor.
  9. Last chance: no 6? The last die is rerolled in slow motion: a fair 1/6 (16.7%) plus your wheel luck of (100 − wheel) ÷ 10 %. Wheel 1 = 26.6%, wheel 99 = 16.8%, wheel 100+ = 16.7%.
  10. 3s, once unlocked, are the last way out: still no 6? Sacrifice ALL your 3s (destroyed for good) to reroll every other die. New 3s can be sacrificed again.
  11. Hand-rollers: roll every die yourself (insta-autoroll never on this game) and the house rewards you: your last die each round is the golden CLUTCH TAP and feels luckier, your last chance gets luckier too, and once per game a round you would lose is played again.
  12. Every 5 floors the elevator gives you one more red die.
  13. Casino Manager: from 10 dice a bad d20 sits on your table, one more for every 5 dice (15, 20, …). They roll themselves. A 1 destroys a red die for good (from floor 25 one more losing face every 18 floors: 1–2, 1–3, …). A 20 confiscates one of your unlocked bonuses.
  14. Chips each round: the sum of your red dice, a round bonus (+10 per round after the first), combos (triple +30, four of a kind +80, five +200, six or more +500 and up, a 1–6 straight +100, six or more 6s in one round +666), the d4 shelf, random casino events, and the falling dice you tap (a rare golden one gives +5 multiplier).
  15. Score = chips × multiplier. At the end you roll the END DIE, a d(total sixes) that adds to the multiplier, plus one more end die for every 10 floors (floor 30 = 4 end dice). Your first end die higher than half of it (rounded up: 7+ on a d12)? You play one more round. Each time you're back at the end die the bar rises: 2/3, then 3/4, 4/5 and so on.
  16. One game every 8 hours. High scores go on this device's leaderboard (🏆).
